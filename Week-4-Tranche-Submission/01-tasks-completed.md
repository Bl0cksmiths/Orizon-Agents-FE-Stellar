| **5.01 — escrow v2 and the settlement path**        | The v1 escrow could never settle a payment between two different wallets (defect D-039, [contracts #3](https://github.com/Bl0cksmiths/Orizon-Agents-Smart-Contract-Stellar/issues/3)). **PaymentEscrow v2** fixes it at the root: `authorize` takes custody of the buyer's authorized amount under the buyer's own signature; `settle` pays each step's on-chain owner and returns the rest to the buyer in the same call; `reclaim` lets the buyer take back an unspent authorization after it expires; and `set_settler` lets the admin rotate the settling key, which v1 could not. The backend now settles every paid run through v2 with one `settle` that pays only the delivered steps, releases custody on every path that does not settle, and binds each authorization to the caller and the plan it was signed for, so one signature buys one task. The console's copy says what the signature does — the plan's maximum moves into escrow and the unused part comes back — and the receipt shows the per-step payouts with their Stellar Expert links. ### Accurate public figures

The console and the home page used to show placeholder statistics. This week they were replaced with measured ones, everywhere: the backend's overview now reports only values it can trace to a source — the registry mirror, the settlement store, the on-chain reputation ledger — and returns `null` with `degraded: true` for anything it cannot read, rather than a guess ([BE #103](https://github.com/Bl0cksmiths/Orizon-Agents-BE-Stellar/pull/103)). The registry mirror now reports when it is fully synced ([BE #113](https://github.com/Bl0cksmiths/Orizon-Agents-BE-Stellar/pull/113)), so the home page's hero shows all three of its figures or none, and never a partial count while the mirror refills after a restart ([FE #113](https://github.com/Bl0cksmiths/Orizon-Agents-FE-Stellar/pull/113), [FE #121](https://github.com/Bl0cksmiths/Orizon-Agents-FE-Stellar/pull/121)). Read at 08:08 UTC on 2026-10-03, the hero and the API agree: **590 registered agents, 565 external agents, 560 operator wallets** (screenshots [10](./screenshots/10-orizons-home-live-stats.png) and [19](./screenshots/19-be-metrics-overview.png)).
