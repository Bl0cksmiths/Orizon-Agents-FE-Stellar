| **5.01 — escrow v2 and the settlement path**        | The v1 escrow could never settle a payment between two different wallets (defect D-039, [contracts #3](https://github.com/Bl0cksmiths/Orizon-Agents-Smart-Contract-Stellar/issues/3)). **PaymentEscrow v2** fixes it at the root: `authorize` takes custody of the buyer's authorized amount under the buyer's own signature; `settle` pays each step's on-chain owner and returns the rest to the buyer in the same call; `reclaim` lets the buyer take back an unspent authorization after it expires; and `set_settler` lets the admin rotate the settling key, which v1 could not. The backend now settles every paid run through v2 with one `settle` that pays only the delivered steps, releases custody on every path that does not settle, and binds each authorization to the caller and the plan it was signed for, so one signature buys one task. The console's copy says what the signature does — the plan's maximum moves into escrow and the unused part comes back — and the receipt shows the per-step payouts with their Stellar Expert links. Deployed to testnet on 2026-09-30 and switched on the same day. Two acceptance runs then proved the hardest cases live: **a dispute that survives a backend restart** (AC4) and **partial delivery**, where a step that hangs is not paid and its share goes back to the buyer (AC5), using a reference agent that can now be told to fail on purpose. | Contracts [#4](https://github.com/Bl0cksmiths/Orizon-Agents-Smart-Contract-Stellar/pull/4), [#6](https://github.com/Bl0cksmiths/Orizon-Agents-Smart-Contract-Stellar/pull/6); BE [#88](https://github.com/Bl0cksmiths/Orizon-Agents-BE-Stellar/pull/88), [#96](https://github.com/Bl0cksmiths/Orizon-Agents-BE-Stellar/pull/96), [#97](https://github.com/Bl0cksmiths/Orizon-Agents-BE-Stellar/pull/97), [#99](https://github.com/Bl0cksmiths/Orizon-Agents-BE-Stellar/pull/99)–[#102](https://github.com/Bl0cksmiths/Orizon-Agents-BE-Stellar/pull/102); FE [#89](https://github.com/Bl0cksmiths/Orizon-Agents-FE-Stellar/pull/89), [#101](https://github.com/Bl0cksmiths/Orizon-Agents-FE-Stellar/pull/101), [#102](https://github.com/Bl0cksmiths/Orizon-Agents-FE-Stellar/pull/102); agent [#6](https://github.com/Bl0cksmiths/Orizon-Agents-Example-Agent-Stellar/pull/6) |
